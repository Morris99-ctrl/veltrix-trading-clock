Manual Windows GUI checklist
1. Launch the standalone application and open ALERTS & EVENTS.
2. Confirm the Alert Intelligence Center appears above the existing Market Watch.
3. Confirm ACTIVE rows and current upcoming session transitions are labelled correctly.
4. Confirm scheduled/moved calendar events inside the next 24 hours appear as UPCOMING context.
5. Confirm status, market, impact, category and timeframe filters plus search update the rows and counters.
6. Select an alert and confirm source/category/detail text is readable.
7. Acknowledge one triggered alert. Confirm it moves to ACKNOWLEDGED.
8. Restart the application and confirm acknowledged history remains.
9. Trigger or test an existing alert and confirm the same stable ID does not produce duplicate history, sound or Windows toast after a restart.
10. Confirm upcoming rows do not send an early notification; existing lead-time settings still control notification timing.
11. Confirm individual session lead times, risk controls, sound, Windows notifications and TEST NOTIFICATION continue to work.
12. Switch through CYAN, AMBER, MATRIX, VIOLET, ICE and LIGHT and inspect contrast, Treeview tags, selectors and row selection.
13. Confirm the existing 28-pair Market Watch, economic calendar and expandable ALERT RULES / PREFERENCES remain available.

Data and safety checks
- No broker feed, order execution or automated trading is introduced.
- Cached provider events remain clearly contextual and cannot bypass the live-reminder freshness guard.
- Session hours are configured schedules; they are not guaranteed broker hours or executable availability.
- A passing unit suite does not prove Windows rendering, provider uptime or trading profitability.
