DATA / SCHEDULE LIMITATIONS
- The 28-pair watchlist is a generic FX trading-week reference only:
  Sunday 17:00 to Friday 17:00 America/New_York.
- It is not a quote feed, broker instrument catalogue, execution-status service,
  or measure of liquidity. Broker-specific hours, rollover breaks, holidays,
  symbol availability, spreads and market energy are not modelled.
- Economic-news events come from the existing ShowMyTrades public feed. Cached
  snapshots are not presented as fresh live data. Verify critical releases
  against their official issuers.
