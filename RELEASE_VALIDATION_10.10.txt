DATA / METHODOLOGY LIMITATIONS
- Session returns use first observed bar open to last observed close.
- Rolling windows count qualifying session observations; excluded days are not
  included in the rolling sample.
- Consistency score = 50 + 50 * mean(return) / mean(abs(return)), bounded 0..100.
- It is not a Sharpe ratio, risk-adjusted statistic, profitability probability,
  forecast or trading signal.
- Regime labels use causal current/past OHLC classification. Regime Lab
  forward-return outcomes are not used for attribution.
- A session day is assigned to its most frequent classified bar label; ties go
  to its latest classified bar. Insufficient regime coverage and WARMUP days are
  not included in the regime summary.
- Coverage estimates are bar-count ratios based on the imported median interval.
- The configured weekday/session profile is used; exchange exceptions, broker
  hours, costs and execution effects are not separately applied.
