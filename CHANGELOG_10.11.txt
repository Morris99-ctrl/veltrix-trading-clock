VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK v10.11 — CHRONOLOGICAL HOLDOUT VALIDATION
Release date: 24 November 2026

CHRONOLOGICAL SESSION VALIDATION
- Added a date-ordered training / holdout split for accepted session-day returns.
- Default split assigns the earliest 70% of accepted observations to training and
  the latest 30% to the holdout. The training share is configurable from 50% to
  90%; the minimum holdout sample defaults to 5 observations.
- Session ranking is calculated using the training segment's signed consistency
  score, then training mean return. Holdout observations never influence the
  training metrics or ranking.
- The validation table reports training / holdout sample sizes, training mean
  return and consistency score, holdout mean return, positive-day rate,
  compounded gross return and maximum drawdown.
- Ranking and segment metrics are withheld when the training sample is below
  the configured minimum ranking sample or the holdout is below its minimum.
- CSV exports now label accepted daily rows TRAIN or HOLDOUT and include the
  split dates, sample sizes and separate segment metrics.

METHODOLOGY / LIMITATIONS
- The split is chronological, per session, and uses only accepted session-day
  observations after the existing minimum-bars and coverage filters.
- The rank is trained on the earliest segment only; the later holdout is not
  used to select or order sessions.
- This is a single chronological holdout check of descriptive session returns.
  It is not a strategy backtest, rolling walk-forward process, forecast or proof
  of a tradable edge.
- Returns remain gross of spreads, commissions, slippage, financing and
  execution effects. Data gaps, session definitions, regime classification
  settings and structural market changes can affect results.

VALIDATION
- Five additional automated tests cover chronological split boundaries,
  training-only ranking, holdout isolation, sample gating, parameter validation
  and CSV output.
- Automated unit tests and Windows packaging are executed by the Core Edition
  GitHub Actions workflow. Manual Windows GUI review remains required.
