MARKET INTELLIGENCE ENGINE (v10.16)
Open MARKET INTEL for causal per-instrument regime context, annualised 10/20/60-day
realised volatility, ATR, historical expected move and aligned 20/60-day
cross-asset correlations. Each instrument includes the evidence behind its
regime label and the freshness of its underlying observations. Cached values
are labelled as cached. Market breadth remains unavailable until real
constituent-level data is configured; Veltrix does not infer it from proxies.
The workspace reuses the existing market-data feed and does not execute trades.

ALERT INTELLIGENCE CENTER (v10.15)
Alerts & Events now begins with a dedicated alert terminal. It separates ACTIVE
session context, UPCOMING session/calendar reminders, TRIGGERED notifications
and ACKNOWLEDGED history. Filter by status, market/currency, impact or search
text. Select an item to inspect its source and details, then acknowledge it.
Triggered and acknowledged history is saved locally. Stable alert IDs prevent
repeat notifications when the app refreshes or restarts. Upcoming rows are
informational and do not override existing reminder lead times or alert rules.
The original Market Watch, 28-pair FX reference watchlist, news calendar,
individual session lead times, sound and Windows notification settings remain.

TRADING COMMAND CENTER (v10.14)
The Command Center is the default landing page. It brings together the four
market-session states, next transitions, upcoming economic releases, indicative
market/ATR/expected-move snapshots, schedule/volatility cautions and a daily
pre-market checklist. Quick links open the original detailed workspaces.
The checklist is saved locally for each home-timezone date.

VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK v10.16 — CORE EDITION

1. Extract this ZIP into a normal folder.
2. Double-click BUILD_AND_INSTALL.bat.
3. When prompted for an existing Veltrix project/AppData folder, enter:
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\Veltrix Trading Clock
   This migrates retained settings, market/calendar caches, your legacy OHLC
   research cache for the standalone Regime Lab, the Regime dataset file,
   Focus & Goals database and alarm audio where present.
4. Close the old Veltrix instance before copying its SQLite focus database.
5. When setup finishes, launch Veltrix from its Desktop or Start-menu icon.

SESSION PERFORMANCE INTELLIGENCE (v10.10)
Import OHLC CSV once in REGIME LAB, then select the same local dataset in
SESSION PERFORMANCE. v10.10 adds trailing rolling returns, compounded return,
maximum drawdown, a signed consistency score and sample-gated session rankings.
It also groups session-day results by the dominant causal Regime Lab label.
The ranking score is descriptive, not risk-adjusted and not a forecast.
See RELEASE_VALIDATION_10.10.txt for the manual desktop checklist.

MARKET WATCH & ALERTS (v10.8)
Alerts & Events now opens a terminal-style Market Watch dashboard with the
UTC session-hours map, current session status, searchable 28-pair FX reference
watchlist and a date/impact-filtered economic news calendar. The FX table uses
a generic New York Sunday 17:00–Friday 17:00 reference week, not broker quotes
or execution hours. New York DST is accounted for in the countdown.
Older calendar snapshots are labelled cached, not live. Open ALERT RULES /
PREFERENCES to reach the original session alerts, lead times, risk settings
and manual event reminders.

SESSION RISK AWARENESS
Each global session card shows NORMAL, CAUTION or HIGH schedule-risk flags.
Open Alerts & Events to configure separate pre-open/pre-close reminders for each
market and the opening/closing caution windows. Holiday-liquidity flags are
calendar heuristics only, not live volume/spread/order-book measurements.
Use SAVE ALERT SETTINGS to persist your choices.

VS Code is NOT required. Python 3.10+ (64-bit) is needed only for this
one-time local build. The installed app includes its Python runtime.

The Core Edition retains Global Sessions, Alerts & Events, Economic Calendar,
Volatility Lab, Regime Lab, Focus & Goals and the original alarm clock.
Global Sessions has four timezone-aware instrument clocks with numbered hour
markers, 60 graduated minute marks, layered hands and smooth second-hand sweeps.
Each dial includes a live 24-hour session-progress ring, opening/closing markers,
UTC-offset and DAY/NIGHT-cycle readouts. The World Session Dial shows all four
markets on concentric UTC tracks. Click or drag the UTC timeline to inspect which
sessions are scheduled open at a selected minute.
An optional cash-exchange calendar overlay applies published ASX, Tokyo, LSE
and NYSE closures/early closes to clocks, timeline and alerts. It is OFF by
default; see EXCHANGE_CALENDAR_SOURCES_10.4.md for date coverage and limitations.
Choose Current Configuration, Forex Standard, Equities Cash or Extended Monitoring,
or edit a Custom profile with local open/close times and active weekdays. The
dashboard also shows weekly scheduled hours and reconstructed 7/30/90-day
scheduled-overlap summaries. These are configured schedule estimates, not
observed exchange history or official holiday-aware market hours.
Research Lab, Strategy Lab, Tick Research and Data Center are removed.

Regime Lab has its own IMPORT OHLC CSV workflow. Existing regular OHLC
datasets can be migrated from the old research_cache.json. Tick-derived
datasets are left in the old cache and are not imported into Regime Lab.
The old research cache and raw tick-data folder are not deleted.

Provider API keys are no longer required by the Core Edition. The installer
does not copy .env files or raw tick-data folders. See
DESKTOP_INSTALL_GUIDE.md for complete migration and data-location details.
