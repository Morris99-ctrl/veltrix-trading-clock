VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK v10.6 — CORE EDITION

1. Extract this ZIP into a normal folder.
2. Double-click BUILD_AND_INSTALL.bat.
3. When prompted for an existing Veltrix project/AppData folder, enter:
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\Veltrix Trading Clock
   This migrates retained settings, market/calendar caches, your legacy OHLC
   research cache for the standalone Regime Lab, the Regime dataset file,
   Focus & Goals database and alarm audio where present.
4. Close the old Veltrix instance before copying its SQLite focus database.
5. When setup finishes, launch Veltrix from its Desktop or Start-menu icon.

SESSION RISK AWARENESS
Each global session card shows NORMAL, CAUTION or HIGH schedule-risk flags.
Open Alerts & Events to configure separate pre-open/pre-close reminders for each
market and the opening/closing caution windows. Holiday-liquidity flags are
calendar heuristics only, not live volume/spread/order-book measurements.
Use SAVE ALERT SETTINGS to persist your choices.

VS Code is NOT required. Python 3.10+ (64-bit) is needed only for this
one-time local build. The installed app includes its Python runtime.

The Core Edition retains Global Sessions, Alerts & Events, Economic Calendar,
Volatility Lab, Regime Lab, Focus & Goals and the original alarm clock.
Global Sessions has four timezone-aware instrument-style clocks with numbered
hour markers, 60 graduated minute marks, layered hands and smooth second-hand
sweeps, plus digital time readouts, a 24-hour UTC map, overlap bands and transitions.
An optional cash-exchange calendar overlay applies published ASX, Tokyo, LSE
and NYSE closures/early closes to clocks, timeline and alerts. It is OFF by
default; see EXCHANGE_CALENDAR_SOURCES_10.4.md for date coverage and limitations.
Choose Current Configuration, Forex Standard, Equities Cash or Extended Monitoring,
or edit a Custom profile with local open/close times and active weekdays. The
dashboard also shows weekly scheduled hours and reconstructed 7/30/90-day
scheduled-overlap summaries. These are configured schedule estimates, not
observed exchange history or official holiday-aware market hours.
Research Lab, Strategy Lab, Tick Research and Data Center are removed.

Regime Lab has its own IMPORT OHLC CSV workflow. Existing regular OHLC
datasets can be migrated from the old research_cache.json. Tick-derived
datasets are left in the old cache and are not imported into Regime Lab.
The old research cache and raw tick-data folder are not deleted.

Provider API keys are no longer required by the Core Edition. The installer
does not copy .env files or raw tick-data folders. See
DESKTOP_INSTALL_GUIDE.md for complete migration and data-location details.
