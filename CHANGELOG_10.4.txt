VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK — v10.4 / EXCHANGE CALENDAR INTELLIGENCE

OFFICIAL CASH-EXCHANGE EXCEPTIONS
- Added a bundled, source-attributed cash-market exception calendar for ASX, Tokyo Stock Exchange / JPX, London Stock Exchange and NYSE.
- Added full-day closure handling for published exchange holidays.
- Added actual early-close handling for ASX normal-trading cessation at 14:10 Sydney time and NYSE early closes at 13:00 Eastern time.
- Added a distinct LSE CLOSING PROCESS marker for half-days where the exchange says the closing process commences at 12:30 London time; this marker does not falsely force the market to CLOSED at 12:30.
- Added venue-specific publication coverage. Dates outside the source dataset are labelled OUT OF PUBLISHED RANGE.
- Overlay is disabled by default and labelled for cash exchanges so generic forex schedules are not silently treated as cash-equity exchange sessions.
- When enabled, the same overlay affects session cards, the 24-hour timeline, open/close alerts, weekly hours and 7/30/90-day overlap reconstruction.
- Full-day exchange holidays suppress normal session open/close alerts. Early-close and closing-process times can trigger pre-close / process-start alerts.

SCOPE LIMITATIONS
- ASX cash-market calendar only; ASX 24 derivatives calendar is separate.
- JPX market-holiday list is used for the Tokyo Stock Exchange cash-market clock; the OSE/TOCOM derivatives holiday-trading programme is not included.
- LSE data starts 31 August 2026 and covers the published on-exchange business-day exceptions; OTC/SI off-book reporting is not modelled.
- NYSE cash-market holiday and early-close schedule only; extended sessions and product-specific calendars are not modelled.
- Unknown emergency halts, unscheduled outages, broker-specific holidays and security-specific suspensions are not included.
- FX spot is not an exchange-listed cash-equity session. The overlay is opt-in and must not be treated as a universal FX holiday schedule.
