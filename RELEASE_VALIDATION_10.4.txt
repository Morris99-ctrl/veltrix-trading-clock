VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK v10.4 — EXCHANGE CALENDAR INTELLIGENCE VALIDATION

Implemented
- Bundled source-attributed exception calendars for ASX cash, Tokyo Stock Exchange / JPX, London Stock Exchange and NYSE.
- Calendar overlay is opt-in and defaults to disabled.
- Full-day closures suppress session states, timeline windows and regular open/close alerts.
- ASX/NYSE early-close times shorten the session state/timeline and adjust pre-close/close alerts.
- LSE 12:30 half-day notice is modelled as a closing-process marker only; it does not force the market closed at 12:30.
- Venue-specific coverage checks mark unknown dates OUT OF PUBLISHED RANGE.
- Weekly scheduled hours and historical overlap analytics use the same optional exchange exception overlay.
- Added exchange calendar source report with date coverage and product limitations.
- Updated README, desktop guide, quick start, Windows metadata and CI artifact name.

Bundled data source coverage
- ASX cash-market: 2026-01-01 through 2027-12-31.
- Tokyo Stock Exchange / JPX market holidays: 2026-01-01 through 2027-12-31.
- NYSE: 2026-01-01 through 2027-12-31.
- London Stock Exchange: 2026-08-31 through 2027-12-31.
- Checked 23 November 2026. See EXCHANGE_CALENDAR_SOURCES_10.4.md for exact URLs and limitations.
