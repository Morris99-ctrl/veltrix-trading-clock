Market Intelligence regression coverage
- Cached observations remain labelled CACHED and are not represented as freshly updated.
- Partial assets and missing observations have explicit status; absent values remain None/N/A, never zero.
- Causal regime label at a timestamp is unchanged by appending later bars.
- Trend labels expose their supporting efficiency-ratio and ATR-normalised slope evidence.
- Annualised realised-volatility windows apply their sample gates.
- ATR and expected-move metrics remain unavailable until their own sample requirements are satisfied.
- Pearson correlations use aligned daily log-return dates and expose sample counts.
- Correlation is unavailable for insufficient samples and zero-variance series.
- Breadth is unavailable without a constituent universe and is calculated only from supplied constituent bars.
- Naive snapshot-generation timestamps are rejected.

Data and methodology checks
- Regime labels use only completed current/past OHLC observations. Forward outcomes do not feed labels.
- Realised volatility is annualised using the sqrt(252) trading-day convention.
- Correlation is Pearson correlation of date-aligned daily log returns, not causal evidence.
- Breadth requires real constituent-level observations; no proxy-derived breadth is allowed.
- Existing Yahoo Finance chart data remains unofficial and indicative.
- No broker integration, trade recommendation, order placement or automated trading is introduced.
