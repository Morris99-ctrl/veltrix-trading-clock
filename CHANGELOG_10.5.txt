VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK — v10.5 / SESSION RISK AWARENESS

SESSION-SPECIFIC RISK FLAGS
- Added risk_engine.py with transparent per-session risk assessments.
- Each Global Sessions card shows NORMAL, CAUTION or HIGH plus concise warning labels.
- Added full exchange-holiday, early-close, closing-process and out-of-calendar-coverage flags.
- Added pre-holiday and post-holiday liquidity cautions when the optional cash-exchange overlay is enabled.
- Added configurable opening-window, near-close and pre-open schedule flags.
- Risk flags use the selected session profile and the existing exchange-calendar overlay.
- Risk warnings are schedule/calendar heuristics. They do not measure actual volume, bid/ask spread, order-book depth, slippage or volatility and do not forecast price direction.

PER-SESSION REMINDERS
- Added separate pre-open and pre-close reminder lead times for Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York.
- Per-session lead times are independent and initially populated from the global defaults.
- Added configurable risk notifications, holiday-liquidity cautions, opening/closing caution windows and pre-open flag window.
- Alert settings and risk preferences persist in config.json.

ENGINEERING AND TESTS
- Added risk_engine.py for deterministic session-risk assessment and deduplicated notifications.
- Updated alert_engine.py to apply per-session reminder overrides while retaining global defaults.
- Updated main.py with risk badges on each session card, the Session Risk Awareness controls and risk notifications.
- Updated config_store.py to validate session_risk preferences.
- Added tests for pre/post-holiday cautions, exchange holiday severity, opening/closing windows, independent per-market reminder leads, risk-notification deduplication and persistence.

DOCUMENTATION AND RELEASE
- Updated README, desktop installation guide and quick start to v10.5.
- Added CHANGELOG_10.5.txt and RELEASE_VALIDATION_10.5.txt.
- Updated Windows executable metadata and CI artifact name to v10.5.

SCOPE LIMITATIONS
- Holiday-liquidity labels mean that liquidity may be thinner around an exchange holiday; they are cautionary heuristics, not observed liquidity conditions.
- Opening/closing windows are based on configured schedule times, not measured volatility.
- No market-depth, trade-volume, spread, execution-quality or broker-specific risk feed is added.
- FX spot is decentralised and does not inherit a universal exchange holiday calendar. Exchange-specific holiday cautions require the optional v10.4 cash-exchange overlay.

VALIDATION
- v10.5 must pass compileall, the unit-test matrix on Python 3.10/3.11/3.12 and Windows PyInstaller packaging.
- Manual Windows review must verify card labels, all six themes, per-session reminder persistence, risk-setting persistence, notification deduplication and minimum-window layout.
