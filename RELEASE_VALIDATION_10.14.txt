10. Confirm the pre-existing alarm, alerts, session clocks, calendar, Volatility Lab, Regime Lab, Session Performance and Focus & Goals still work.

Data limitations
- Market quotes and volatility statistics retain the existing unofficial Yahoo Finance source limitations.
- Economic events retain the existing public provider, cache and coverage limitations.
- Session status is based on configured schedules with optional published cash-exchange calendar overlay.
- This dashboard is a monitoring and preparation surface, not a forecast or trading system.
