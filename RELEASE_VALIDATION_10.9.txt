DATA / METHODOLOGY LIMITATIONS
- CSV bar timestamps are treated as bar-open times.
- Naive timestamps were interpreted in the selected IANA home timezone during
  the original import; offset-aware timestamps are converted to UTC.
- Session classification uses the active profile's local weekday/open/close
  conventions. Exchange holidays, early closes, broker-specific hours and
  maintenance breaks are not separately applied.
- Session coverage is estimated by observed bars / expected bars from the
  median imported interval. It is not proof of complete minute-level coverage.
- Realised volatility is not annualised. Returns do not include transaction costs.
- This is historical description, not a forecast or trade recommendation.
