SCHEDULE LIMITATIONS
- Timeline windows represent configured weekday schedules only. Official market holidays, emergency closures, broker hours, instrument-specific hours and overnight sessions are not modelled.
- The timeline is not a live exchange-status feed.

No trading profitability or live market availability claim is made.
