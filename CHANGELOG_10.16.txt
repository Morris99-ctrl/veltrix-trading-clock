VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK v10.16 — MARKET INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

Overview
- Adds a dedicated MARKET INTEL workspace using the existing market snapshot and configured instruments.
- Extends the current Regime Lab and Volatility Lab foundations without replacing their workflows.

Causal regime context
- Shows the latest per-instrument RANGE, TREND_UP, TREND_DOWN, VOLATILITY_SHOCK, TRANSITION or WARMUP state.
- Reuses the established efficiency-ratio, ATR-normalised regression-slope and ATR-shock classification.
- Displays efficiency ratio, normalised slope, fast/slow ATR ratio and true range versus prior ATR as evidence.
- Current regime labels use completed current/past OHLC bars only. Future returns remain separate historical outcome diagnostics.

Volatility profile
- Adds annualised 10-, 20- and 60-observation realised volatility from daily log returns using the sqrt(252) convention.
- Adds a rolling 20-day volatility percentile against up to 60 rolling 20-day observations.
- Shows ATR(14), ATR as a percentage of price, and the existing one-day 1-sigma expected-move estimate.
- Sample gates leave unavailable statistics as N/A rather than fabricating values.

Cross-asset relationship monitor
- Adds 20- and 60-observation Pearson correlations from aligned daily log returns for configured instruments.
- Shows sample counts for both windows. Insufficient samples and zero-variance pairs remain N/A.
- The panel is descriptive correlation context, not causal evidence or a forecast.

Market breadth and data quality
- Market breadth is UNAVAILABLE unless a real constituent-level OHLC universe is explicitly supplied.
- Index/futures proxies are not treated as market breadth.
- Feed status distinguishes UPDATED, PARTIAL, CACHED and NO DATA; stale cached observations are never labelled as newly updated.
- The refresh button reuses the existing non-blocking market-data worker and adds no provider.

Preserved behaviour and boundaries
- Preserves v10.15 Alert Intelligence Center, v10.14 Trading Command Center, global session clocks, calendar, Volatility Lab, Regime Lab, Session Performance, Focus & Goals and original alarm controls.
- No new API provider, broker connection, buy/sell signal, order placement or trading automation is introduced.
- Yahoo Finance public chart observations remain unofficial and indicative, not execution-grade market data.
- The workspace is research and decision-support context, not a profitability claim.
