VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK 7.3

CUSTOM BROKER INSTRUMENT IMPORT
- Added IMPORT AS NEW INSTRUMENT to Research Lab.
- Broker CSV datasets are registered as persistent selectable instruments.
- Custom datasets appear in both Research Lab and Strategy Lab after import and on subsequent launches.
- Existing selected-instrument CSV replacement remains available.
- Re-importing an instrument with the same name updates its existing custom dataset key.
- Custom strategy datasets explicitly warn users to verify point value, quantity step, contract size and transaction costs.
- Added selector/key helper tests.

Compatibility
- Existing built-in market instruments and caches are unchanged.
- Custom research instruments are stored in research_cache.json and are not added to live market-data instruments.
