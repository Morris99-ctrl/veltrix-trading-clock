VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK v10.5 — SESSION RISK AWARENESS VALIDATION

Implemented
- Added per-session risk assessment with NORMAL, CAUTION and HIGH levels.
- Added risk labels to the four Global Sessions cards.
- Added exchange holiday, early-close, closing-process, schedule-only, pre-holiday and post-holiday flags.
- Added opening-window, near-close and pre-open schedule flags.
- Added one-time risk notifications keyed by session/date/flag.
- Added independent Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York pre-open/pre-close reminder lead times.
- Added user controls for risk flags, risk notifications, holiday-liquidity cautions, opening-window minutes, closing-window minutes and pre-open flag minutes.
- Added persistence and configuration validation for session_risk settings and session lead overrides.
- Updated README, desktop installation guide, quick start, changelog, Windows metadata and CI artifact name.

Important interpretation
- Holiday-liquidity flags are calendar heuristics only. They do not measure actual liquidity, volume, spread, order-book depth, slippage or volatility.
- Opening/closing window flags are based on scheduled local session times. They do not predict market direction or actual realised volatility.
- Holiday-specific warnings require the optional v10.4 cash-exchange overlay. With the overlay disabled, risk assessment uses the configured local session schedule only.

Required automated checks
1. Run: python -m compileall -q .
2. Run: python -m unittest discover -s tests -v.
3. Run the Python 3.10, 3.11 and 3.12 GitHub Actions matrix.
4. Build the Windows desktop package and verify executable/artifact upload.

Required manual Windows checks
1. Inspect risk labels on all four session cards in all six themes.
2. Verify NORMAL, CAUTION and HIGH states around normal opens/closes, holidays and early closes.
3. Verify pre-holiday caution on the configured trading day before a published closure.
4. Verify post-holiday caution on the first configured session after a published closure.
5. Change Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York pre-open/pre-close reminder leads independently; save, restart and verify persistence.
6. Change opening, closing and pre-open risk windows; save, restart and verify persistence.
7. Disable card flags, risk notifications and holiday-liquidity cautions independently and verify each control's behavior.
8. Verify risk notifications fire once per session/date/flag during an application run.
9. Verify the exchange overlay OFF/ON behavior and the existing SCHEDULE ONLY state outside published coverage.
10. Test the Alerts page layout and Global Sessions card wrapping at normal/minimum window sizes.

No claim of live liquidity measurement, predictive volatility, trade execution suitability or profitability is made.
