VELTRIX TRADING CLOCK — PHASE 8.0
Multi-Resolution & Information-Driven Bar Engine

Added
- Raw tick CSV import with timestamp parsing, stable sorting, duplicate preservation and row validation. The bounded two-pass importer avoids a second full in-memory copy of large exports; a 250,000-bar output cap protects the research cache.
- Last-price tick-rule classification when sufficiently available; otherwise Bid/Ask midpoint proxy with explicit provenance label.
- Fixed-tick bar construction with trailing incomplete-tick reporting.
- Adaptive Tick Imbalance Bars (TIB) with seed/calibration bar, separately configured EWMA expected bar length (bars) and tick-by-tick signed imbalance (ticks), plus a configurable minimum expected-imbalance floor.
- Raw-source timezone is captured at import and reused for every subsequent bar build, preventing accidental timestamp reinterpretation when the UI timezone setting changes.
- Raw tick source registry in research_cache.json and local source preservation under tick_data/.
- Separate generated OHLC datasets selectable from Research Lab and Strategy Lab.
- Variable-interval session research handling propagated from generated-dataset metadata, plus sampling provenance in Strategy Lab status / HTML export.
- Background processing for raw tick validation and bar construction.

Preserved
- Existing time-based/provider OHLC datasets and CSV imports.
- Existing alarm, themes, global sessions, alerts, economic calendar and volatility workspace.
- Existing SMA/ATR Strategy Lab, next-bar-open execution, costs, holdout and walk-forward evaluation.

Important limitations
- Tick direction is inferred with the tick rule; it is not an exchange aggressor-side flag.
- Bid/Ask midpoint bars are quote-flow proxies, not true trade-flow TIBs.
- Volume imbalance bars are not implemented because broker volume semantics must first be verified.
- Strategy Lab consumes the resulting OHLC bars; it does not perform raw tick replay or model the exact intrabar order path.
- Broker point value, contract size, quantity step, spread, commissions and slippage must be configured and verified before interpreting any backtest.
